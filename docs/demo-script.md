# Demo script (about 4 minutes)

**0:00 Intro (20s).** "A roster tool for 34 people, 4 pods, 24 brands. Brands run on different calendars, so the hard part is coverage, not the calendar."

**0:20 Generation (50s).** Dashboard: pick November 2026, show the four plans in the strategy selector, press Regenerate. Point at coverage %, fairness score, the 2 planted errors.

**1:10 Different brand schedules (50s).** Roster page. Company view, then Brand view: a 365-day brand (Zest) vs a Sunday-off brand. Process view: Zenith needs 2 people on weekends. Pod and Employee views in one sentence each.

**2:00 Conflict detection (40s).** Conflicts page: Zest has no cover on Sun 15 Nov, Zenith is understaffed on Sat 21 Nov. Press Explain to show the plain-language reason. Click "Use ..." on a ranked cover; the error clears.

**2:40 Handling a change (50s).** Simulate a change > Load demo (Sneha, 17-18 Nov, sole owner of Pixel Wear). Show the gap, then Apply and re-solve week: only that week changes, locked edits stay.

**3:30 Close (30s).** Manual edit by clicking a cell. Compare page. Approval: switch role to a pod lead, approve; operations manager publishes. Edit after publishing triggers the notification. "Rules and brands are all data, added in Setup."
